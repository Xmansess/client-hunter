# Scoring scales

| Factor | Scale |
|---|---|
| **fit** (0 to 3) | 3: squarely what an offer delivers, for the kind of client the profile names. 2: fits with a small gap (scope, location, platform) the user can bridge honestly. 1: adjacent; would need a stretch. 0: not what the user does. |
| **intent** (0 to 3) | 3: wants a provider now (hiring post, "looking for a sourcing agent"). 2: evaluating how or whom to hire. 1: describes a pain only. 0: chatter or curiosity. |
| **budget** (0 to 2) | 2: stated budget at or above the offer minimum, or clear spending power (company, inventory, volumes). 1: no budget stated but plausible (a business with a real product). 0: stated below minimum, or a hobbyist with no means. |
| **fresh** (0 to 2) | Reddit, Telegram, X, LinkedIn: 2 under 24 h, 1.5 under 48 h, 1 under 4 days, 0.5 under 7 days, 0 older. Forums and tenders: 2 under 3 days, 1 under 7, 0.5 under 14, 0 older; for tenders use time to deadline instead (2 if over 5 days left, 1 if 2 to 5, 0 if under 2). |
| **competition** (0 to 2) | 2: no useful replies yet. 1.5: a few replies, none substantive. 1: some solid replies, room for a better one. 0.5: many strong replies or an accepted solution. 0: closed, filled or answered. "DM me" replies from other vendors count as weak. |
| **reach** (0 to 1) | 1: at least one allowed channel where the user can be genuinely useful. 0.5: public reply only, with no-promotion rules. 0: no allowed channel (locked thread, DMs and promo banned, and nothing helpful to add). |
| **value** (0 to 2) | 2: likely above twice the offer minimum, or ongoing work. 1: around the minimum, one-off. 0: below minimum. |

Tier rules: A if total >= 11 and fit >= 2 and intent >= 2 and verdict pass. B if not A and total >= 8 (this includes A-level leads with verdict caution, or with fit or intent below 2). C if total < 8.

Worked example (Nov 2026 r/smallbusiness post "Looking for a reliable sourcing agent in Shenzhen"; profile offers remote sourcing and supplier evaluation, no on-site work):
- fit 1 for now: remote supplier search and comparison fit, but the post wants factory visits and inspection in Shenzhen. Question for the user: "Would you run the remote side and partner with an on-site QC firm?" If yes, fit becomes 2.
- intent 3 (wants a long-term agent now), budget 1 (brand launch, no budget stated), fresh 2 (hours old), competition 1 (7 replies: 3 are "DM me" agents, 2 are substantive), reach 0.5 (the subreddit is Q&A with self-promotion restricted, so a genuinely helpful public answer is the channel; a DM only if the poster invites it), value 2 (long-term project).
- Total 10.5 with fit 1: tier B. If the user answers yes, fit 2 gives 11.5 and tier A.
